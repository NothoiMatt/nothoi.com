SMS Messaging Policy

Program Description

Nothoi LLC operates a private notification and executive briefing service. Registered contacts receive automated SMS messages including system status alerts, daily briefings, task confirmations, scheduling updates, and operational notifications. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

Who Can Subscribe

Nothoi LLC's SMS service is available to invited clients and contacts. You must be 18 or older.

How to Opt In

The only way to subscribe is to send a text message from your own mobile phone. There is no web form. By texting the keyword you confirm that you agree to receive automated text messages from Nothoi LLC. You understand that:

  • Message frequency: up to 10 messages per week
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Consent is not required as a condition of any purchase
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message
  • You can request help by replying HELP to any message

Your mobile number will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time.
